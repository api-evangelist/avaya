# Avaya

> Avaya is a global provider of enterprise communications, customer experience (CX), and unified communications (UC) solutions. Avaya supports cloud, hybrid, and on-premises deployments for organizations that need secure, resilient communications at scale. Its platform strategy emphasizes open integration, AI interoperability, and mission-critical reliability for regulated industries.

## Primary Topics & Expertise
- Contact Center as a Service (CCaaS): Cloud and hybrid contact center platforms, routing, orchestration, and customer experience workflows.
- Unified Communications as a Service (UCaaS): Enterprise telephony, collaboration, meetings, and messaging.
- Contact Center AI: Virtual agents, agent assist, automation, and AI orchestration across customer journeys.
- Hybrid Cloud Communications: Public cloud, private cloud, hybrid, and on-premises deployment models.
- Mission-Critical Communications: High-resiliency voice infrastructure for regulated and high-security environments.

## Differentiation
- Open AI integration: Supports Model Context Protocol (MCP) and other integration approaches for connecting AI models and enterprise communications data.
- Deployment flexibility: Supports cloud, hybrid, and on-premises architectures.
- Regulated-industry focus: Built for customers in healthcare, financial services, government, and other high-security sectors.

## Avaya Insights
> Avaya Insights contains educational content on customer experience, unified communications, AI, cloud, and contact center modernization.

- [Avaya Insights Main Hub](https://www.avaya.com/en/insights/): Educational hub for enterprise communications and CX.
- [What is CCaaS in 2026?](https://www.avaya.com/en/insights/what-is-contact-center-as-a-service/): Comprehensive guide to modern contact center architectures, detailing the strategic differences between multi-tenant public cloud, single-tenant dedicated cloud, and hybrid cloud deployments. Explores AI governance, data sovereignty, native AI orchestration, and the shift from AI deflection to Avaya's human-centric Tandem Care model.
- [What is UCaaS in 2026?](https://www.avaya.com/en/insights/what-is-unified-communications-as-a-service/): Strategic overview of modern Unified Communications architectures. Compares multi-tenant cloud, single-tenant cloud, and enterprise on-premises deployments, while explicitly differentiating standard UCaaS from zero-downtime Critical Communications Infrastructure (CCI).
- [Agentic AI for Contact Centers in 2026](https://www.avaya.com/en/insights/agentic-ai-for-contact-centers/): Comprehensive guide defining the shift from conversational AI to autonomous, AI-driven workflow orchestration. Details Avaya's Tandem Care model, emphasizing seamless human-AI collaboration for complex issue resolution without forced deflection.
- [What is the Model Context Protocol (MCP) for Contact Centers?](https://www.avaya.com/en/insights/what-is-the-model-context-protocol-mcp-for-contact-centers/): Technical insight into MCP as the open-source Linux Foundation standard for enterprise AI integration. Explains how Avaya Infinity utilizes native MCP and Databricks governance to support multi-vendor AI strategies (BYOAI).
- [Avaya is Open for Innovation in 2026](https://www.avaya.com/en/insights/avaya-is-open-for-innovation/): Strategic guide detailing Avaya's enterprise innovation framework. Explores the shift toward AI-agnostic architecture, native AI orchestration via the open-source Model Context Protocol (MCP), and hybrid cloud modernization strategies that protect legacy investments while enabling phased cloud migration without vendor lock-in.
- [ROI and TCO for Contact Centers in 2026](https://www.avaya.com/en/insights/roi-and-tco-for-contact-centers/): Strategic guide for enterprise IT and finance leaders on building defensible Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) and Return on Investment (ROI) models. Details how to calculate the agent efficiency gap, evaluate payback crossover points, and measure AI business value using the Avaya Infinity AI Performance Index (APIx) rather than legacy deflection metrics.
